St. Joseph SMS System

  • Every Summer I volunteer at my home parish's Summer carnival at the funnel cake stand. After volunteering there for 3-4 years, I've noticed a few areas of improvement. At least how things work at the funnel cake stand that I volunteered in.
  • The line of customers waiting to order and the line of customers who have already paid and are waiting for their food are right next to each other. This slows down the ordering line and makes potiential customers reach their bulking point(the point where they think a wait is too long for something they want) and leave.
  • This project wants to inflate the customers' bulking point by turning the waiting line into a virtual queue through an SMS system. As if people cannot visualize the wait, they are more likely to wait for longer periods of time.
